---
name: Installation team editor
description: Product expectation for managing growing Installation team rosters.
---

**Rule:** Keep Installation team creation and editing on the teams page rather than in a popup. Member pickers should remain searchable and the page should support normal vertical scrolling.

**Why:** The user expects the existing-user roster to grow beyond what a popup can comfortably display.

**How to apply:** Preserve the page-level editor when changing team management. Do not reintroduce a constrained dialog for team or subdivision membership.
